How OLFA works

  1. Create your profile

    Your phone number, then your photos and an honest bio about you and what you are looking for.

  2. Verify with a selfie

    A live photo with a gesture we ask for, reviewed against eight written criteria and deleted 30 days after the decision.

  3. Answer the compatibility questions

    Values, lifestyle and expectations of marriage — used to suggest the people who suit you best.

  4. Connect respectfully

    Chat after a mutual like, with the option to blur your photos until a match, and a counselor to talk to whenever you wish.

Safety is not an add-on

Human review

Every profile and photo is reviewed before it appears, by a female reviewer on request for the selfie.

Report and block in one tap

Report any profile or message; our team handles reports with priority.

Protected conversations

Screenshots are blocked on Android and content is hidden during screen recording on iOS — no system can stop someone photographing a screen with another camera.

Clear rules

Temporary or permanent bans for breaking the Terms, with the right to appeal.

Relationship and family counselors

Book a session with a verified psychologist or family counselor. The fee is held and only paid to the counselor after you confirm the session took place and rate it — if it did not happen, a dispute opens for our team to review.

Questions

Is the app available now?

Not yet. We are working on releasing it on the App Store and Google Play and will announce it here when it is available.

Who is OLFA for?

Adults (18+) who are seriously looking for marriage.

Is the selfie check an ID check?

No. It confirms the account belongs to a real person who matches their photos; it does not replace your own care and checks before any commitment.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. What is deleted and when is described in the Privacy Policy.
