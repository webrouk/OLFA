  • You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher).

  • You must not be prohibited from using the service by law, and you must not have been removed from OLFA before.
  • You confirm that you are seeking a serious relationship and are legally able to marry where you live.

2. Account types

When you sign up you choose one account type, which cannot be changed later: a member looking for marriage, a psychologist, or a family counselor. Professional accounts never appear in discovery, cannot like or match, and use the counselor workspace (requests, wallet, chats with clients) under the Professional Policy. A person who is both looks for marriage with a member account and offers consultations with a separate professional account.

2a. Your account

  • Keep your login secure; you are responsible for activity on your account.

  • Information you provide must be true and current. Photos must show you and you must have the right to use them.

5. Matching, messages and safety

  • Profiles are shown only to members whose preferences match yours and vice versa.
  • We do not run criminal background checks and cannot guarantee anyone's identity or intentions. Verification badges mean only what they describe.
  • Meet in public places, tell someone you trust, never send money, and report anything suspicious.
  • Conversations with no messages for 15 days are removed from your list (we remind you after 10 days); archived conversations are kept for 30 days; unmatched conversations are deleted after 30 days. One re-match request is allowed per conversation.

6. Subscriptions and in-app purchases

  • OLFA Plus and Premium are auto-renewing subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is charged to your store account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Moving up to Premium takes effect immediately (the store credits the unused part of the current period). Moving down to Plus takes effect at your next renewal date; you keep Premium until then.
  • If a renewal payment fails, the store retries it; during the store's grace period you keep your plan, after that it is paused until the payment is fixed.
  • Restore purchases (on the Plans screen) re-links an active subscription bought with the same App Store or Google Play account.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a store subscription.
  • Prices are shown in the app before purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Plan limits (likes, conversation starters, visible conversations, compliments) are shown in the app and enforced by our servers.

7. Family consultations

Consultations are a person-to-person service between you and an independent licensed counselor. You pay before the request is sent; if the counselor declines, or you cancel before confirmation, you are refunded in full. OLFA keeps a 15% platform commission on each consultation; the rest is the counselor's. After each completed session you answer a short questionnaire about its quality and about any attempt to move sessions or payments outside OLFA; you need to answer it before booking again, and your answers are confidential. Never pay a counselor outside OLFA — payments outside the app are not protected. Counselors are reviewed but are responsible for their own professional advice. Consultations are not emergency or medical care — in an emergency, contact local emergency services.

8. Freezing or deleting your account

  • Freeze (take a break): your profile is hidden from everyone and your data, matches, chats and subscription are kept until you return.
  • Delete: from Settings › Delete account. Your account is hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days; signing in during that time cancels the deletion.

A permanently banned person may not open a new account; to enforce this we keep one-way hashes of the phone number, device, licence and payout account of the banned account (never the values themselves). You can contact support from Help & support to ask about or appeal a decision.
