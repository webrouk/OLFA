Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Version 2026-11

This policy explains what OLFA collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

1. What we collect

  • Account: phone number (verified by SMS code), device identifier and type, app version, language and time zone, and your account type (member or professional).
  • Profile: name, date of birth, gender, country of residence, nationality, city, marital status, education, occupation, height and weight (optional), marriage timeline, bio, interests, photos and partner preferences.
  • Religion and sect (only with your explicit consent): you can choose "Prefer not to say". They are shown on your profile and used only as filters that you or other members set; they never affect compatibility scores.
  • Professionals: payout account details, sealed with encryption and readable only by our finance team when they send a payout; your earnings ledger.
  • Consultation questionnaires: your answers after each session, kept confidential and used only for quality and to protect clients.
  • Approximate location (only if you allow it): rounded to about 1 km to show nearby members first. Others see only an approximate distance — never your location.
  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, messages, compliments, reports and blocks.
  • Purchases: subscription status from Apple or Google; for consultations, payment status from our payment provider (we never receive or store full card numbers).
  • Counselors: licence details and documents, kept private and seen only by our review team.
  • Technical: hashed IP address, crash and performance data, and security logs.

2. Why we use it

To create and secure your account; to show your profile only to compatible members; to run discovery, matching, chat and consultations; to enforce plan limits; to review profiles and photos and keep the community safe; to prevent fraud and abuse; to send notifications you can control; and to meet legal obligations. Sensitive details you choose to share (for example your religion or nationality) are used only to provide the service and are never sold.

3. What other members see

Your public profile: name, age, photos (after review), city and distance (unless you hide them), nationality, bio, interests and the details you add. Never your phone number, exact birth date, exact location or account status. You control discovery visibility, incognito (Premium), blurred photos until match, online status and read receipts in Privacy & Security.

4. Who we share it with

Service providers acting on our instructions: cloud hosting and storage, SMS delivery, push notifications (Apple, Google Firebase), photo moderation tools, payment processing (Apple, Google, Stripe for consultations) and crash reporting. Authorities, only when required by law or to protect someone's safety. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. How long we keep it

  • Active account: while you use OLFA.
  • Conversations: removed from lists after 15 days without messages; archive 30 days; unmatched 30 days.
  • Frozen account: kept until you return or delete it.
  • Deleted account: hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days, including photos and messages. We keep the minimum needed for legal, tax and safety reasons (for example payment records and records of serious violations) for as long as the law requires.
  • Permanently banned accounts: one-way hashes of the phone number, devices, licence and payout account are kept to block re-registration, for as long as the ban lasts.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: Privacy & Security › Download my data.
  • Correct: edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete: Settings › Delete account (in the app — no email needed).
  • Freeze instead of delete: keep everything and take a break.
  • Withdraw location permission or notifications in your device settings.
  • Contact us to object to or restrict processing where the law gives you that right.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; tokens are stored in the device's secure storage; verification codes and secrets are stored only as hashes; original photos are kept private and only reviewed copies are published. No system is perfectly secure — tell us right away if you think your account is at risk.

8. Children

OLFA is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and remove such accounts when we find them.

9. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than yours, with safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Changes

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: Settings › Help & support › Contact us.
